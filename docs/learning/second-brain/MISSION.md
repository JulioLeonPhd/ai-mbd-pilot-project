# Mission: Recover engineering decisions across sessions

## Why

Resume work in this MATLAB/Simulink project with the right decision, baseline,
and evidence in view, while leaving architectural authority with Julio. This
should help each session continue from approved project records without
overstating what a test or historical result proves.

## Success looks like

- At the start of the next three sessions, identify the current decision, any
  superseded baseline, the scoped evidence for the claim, and the next decision
  requiring Julio.
- Record recovery time and the maintenance cost of keeping the recovery note
  useful; the provisional target is two minutes per session.
- Link back to the authoritative ADRs, status, and evidence instead of creating
  a competing source of truth.

## Constraints

- Treat existing ADRs, status, and evidence as authoritative; label uncertainty
  and keep evidence scopes distinct.
- This is a small three-session experiment. Record observations; do not claim
  measured improvement or learner mastery in advance.

## Out of scope

- The current lesson does not cover installing or selecting an app, migrating a
  vault, changing project architecture, or adding automation.
