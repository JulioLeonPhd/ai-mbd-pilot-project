# Adopt the 3 GHz 16×4 half-wave array baseline

The reference project adopts a 3 GHz baseline (approximately 0.1 m
wavelength) with approximately 0.05 m half-wave spacing and a 16-horizontal by
4-vertical single-panel array. This gives the demonstrator a more useful
azimuth aperture than a 4×4 panel while keeping the elevation dimension small;
the choice is supported by the ideal beam-pattern study in [MATLAB
source](../../evidence/beam_pattern_study.m) and [plot](../../evidence/beam-pattern-study.png),
which reports uniform 3 dB widths of approximately 6.36° at boresight and
9.02° at +45°, and Hamming widths of approximately 9.74° and 13.88° at those
angles. These are ideal array-factor results and do not guarantee coverage,
detection, or angle performance; the 90° azimuth sector remains provisional.
